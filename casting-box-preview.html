The plan being verified

  • Two separate printable boxes, independent of the mold forms: one rectangular box for wall forms, one circular box for the foot form.
  • One box serves all walls — every wall form is identical (shared half-width), so print the wall box once.
  • Form sits outer-face-down on the box floor, nested into a shallow indent that registers it and seals the underside.
  • Consistent gap between the form's sides and the box walls, all the way around.
  • Open top; interior is 1″ taller than the seated form → pour to the brim leaves 1″ of polymer over the form.
